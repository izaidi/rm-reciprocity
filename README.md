# A reciprocity law for quantum dilogarithm values at real multiplication points, with applications to Hilbert's twelfth problem, Stark units and SICs

## Abstract

We prove a reciprocity law for the values of Faddeev's quantum dilogarithm at real multiplication points, which Radchenko and Wheeler showed to be algebraic. For a real quadratic field $K$, each value is attached to a lattice $I$ in $K$, a totally positive unit $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon I=I$, and a torsion point of $K/I$. Assuming one of Radchenko and Wheeler's identities, the Fourier proportionality relation, we show that the squared absolute values lie in the maximal abelian extension $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$, and that each idele of $K$ acts on them by moving the lattice and the torsion point, with an inversion when the idele is negative at the second real place. The proof derives Frobenius congruences from a $p$-adic argument on the Bruhat--Tits tree and globalizes them with Chebotarev's theorem. We deduce that the values attached to the maximal order are units at every finite prime, and that the square roots of all values lie in $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$. Using Shintani's product formula, we show that the values together with $i$ generate $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$. We also prove the rank-one abelian Stark conjecture at a real place of $K$ for $|S|\ge3$, including Tate's square-root condition. In one configuration the square-root condition also uses the Fourier-transform identity of Faddeev, Kashaev and Volkov, and there we prove a stronger root law in most cases. Finally, with a quadratic relation of Radchenko and Wheeler, we obtain Zauner-symmetric SICs (symmetric informationally complete quantum measurements) in every dimension $M\ge5$.
